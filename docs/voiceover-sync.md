# Voice-over sync — awgur

The final video is 2 minutes 40 seconds, silent, with website and installed Android footage. Use [demo-script.md](demo-script.md) beside it.

1. Play the muted video with headphones and record your narration in a quiet room. Keep a half-second of silence before the first sentence.
2. Aim for the nine scene markers in the script. Pause briefly between blocks; keep the sentences connected rather than reading the table headings.
3. Import the video and voice recording into your editor. Put the first spoken “Imagine” at 0:00, then align the Android transition at 0:40, evidence at 1:45 and closing at 2:23. Trim the initial silence.
4. If a block runs long, re-record that block or shorten a pause. Avoid speeding up the entire voice track. Export MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio, keeping the original 1920×1080 picture.

Record the final sentence so it ends by 2:39. The existing video has no music to compete with your voice. You can also record nine separate clips and place each at its marked start.

Footage comes from the public hosted website and a real installed Android debug variant for repeatable capture. It uses the release’s engine and screens; the final delivered APK is separately release-signed. No phone-shaped browser simulation is used.
