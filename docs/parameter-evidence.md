# Parameter evidence

- R1 is a configurable 12-calendar-month demonstration default. The scope of the documented Chennai policy remains unverified; this app does not present it as a citywide law.
- R2 90-day gap, 180-day bundle horizon, 30-day flexibility and 30-day restoration default are explicit demo assumptions.
- The 30-day telecom counter is retained as an illustrative placeholder from the build brief. Source verification on 10 October 2026: Rule 7(1)(b) of the 2024 DoT text specifies a 67-day permission procedure, alongside clarification and reasoned-rejection stages; Rule 7(6) addresses deemed permission. The app does not calculate the legal process.

Primary source: [DoT Telecommunications (Right of Way) Rules, 2024](https://www.eservices.dot.gov.in/sites/default/files/circular-notifications/telecommunications-right-of-way-rules-2024.pdf), English text, page 22 of the Gazette (PDF page index 21).
